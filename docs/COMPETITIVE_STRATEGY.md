# Competitive Strategy (legal synthesis)

Original competitive analysis for our wedding AutoLab editor.
**Not** CapCut / WeddingEditor / Magisto IP, templates, or scraped products.

## Goal

Beat CapCut-style AutoCut and wedding AI editors on **real wedding-film quality**:
emotion, story, pacing, music relationship — not template flash.

## What competitors do well

| Signal | Competitor | Our response |
|--------|------------|--------------|
| Multi-style first assembly | CapCut AutoCut | `autolab/craft/presets/` AutoCut style packs |
| Beat-synced cuts | CapCut beat markers | Phrase / downbeat pacing in `plan_story` (not every-beat spray) |
| Style switch preview | CapCut | Tournament runs many presets; keep winner by evidence |
| Social vs highlight lengths | WeddingEditor.ai | `social_teaser_30` + `highlight_reel_75` |
| Emotion↔music matching | Magisto | Ranking intimacy + section energy fit |
| Hold emotional peaks | Pro wedding style guides | `peak_hold` + craft `peak_min_dur` |

## What we refuse to copy

- Paid CapCut / Envato / MotionArray packs
- Scraped YouTube wedding films
- Course transcripts or proprietary template timelines

## Winning order (quality priority)

1. Emotional impact  
2. Story coherence  
3. Pacing (phrase-level, not choppy)  
4. Music relationship  
5. Visual / continuity polish  
6. Titles / bookends  

## Product modes

- **Social Teaser** — ~30–38s, portrait/couple biased, soft open  
- **Cinematic AutoCut** — long holds, soft dissolves, emotional peak linger  
- **Beat AutoCut** — denser chorus/party cuts, still phrase-floored  
- **Highlight Reel** — ~75s narrative arc  

## Promotion rule

Never promote a preset to editor default on critic score alone.
Require comparable-length critic ≥ baseline **and** human-feel checklist:

1. Emotion lands (not only pretty faces)  
2. No stock-collage jump cuts  
3. Music feels accompanied  
4. Ending resolves  
5. Peak holds breathe  

## 1000-clip test

Rebuild shot pool on the full legal library, tournament all templates + presets,
KEEP/REJECT with comparable ≤42s gate vs V4 emotional 8.36.
