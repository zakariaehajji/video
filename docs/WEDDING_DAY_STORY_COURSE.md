## Ten machine rules (encode these)

1. **Day chronology first** — audience must feel *before → during → after*, not random stock.
2. **Intro establishes world** — wide/detail, soft dissolve, no kiss yet.
3. **Before = prep montage** — rings, dress, makeup, portraits; shorter intellectual montage OK.
4. **During = ceremony continuity** — vows/portraits → rings/detail → kiss/couple; hold emotion.
5. **After = celebration rhythm** — motion, smiles, guests; denser cuts allowed.
6. **Outro resolves** — wide + couple + detail; soft dissolve; fade with music.
7. **Phase boundaries are edit events** — soft into ceremony; hard into party climax.
8. **One emotional trajectory** — soft open → crest in during → breathe in after → resolve.
9. **Titles bookend the film** — not the story itself; story lives in footage phases.
10. **Same graphic frame** — landscape 16:9 fill so orientation doesn’t break immersion.
